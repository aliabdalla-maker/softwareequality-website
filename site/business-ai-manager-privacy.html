Business AI Manager Privacy Policy

Equality Software Ltd · Company No. 15006381 · Last updated: October 2026

1. Who We Are

Business AI Manager (the "app") is provided by Equality Software Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (Co. No. 15006381), Winchester House, 259-269 Old Marylebone Road, 2nd Floor, London NW1 5RA. We are the data controller for personal data processed through the app. Contact: ali.abdalla@softwareequality.com.

This policy applies to Business AI Manager on every platform: the Android app (Google Play package com.softwareequality.smeaimanager, also offered as a direct download), the Windows desktop app, the web app at api.softwareequality.com/app, and the supporting online service. The app is intended for users aged 15 and over (see section 12). It is available in 30 languages, with voice input and spoken replies.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only what the app needs to work:

  • Account data: your name, email address, a securely hashed password, and the language you choose for the app (one of 30)
  • Workspace data: your business name, team memberships and roles
  • Business data you enter or upload: records in the app's business systems (customers and leads, sales, orders, point of sale, messages, finance and accounting, budgets, suppliers and purchasing, employees, leave, timesheets, pay and payroll, training, projects, vehicles, drivers, trips, routes, deliveries, fuel and shipments), files and folders of any type that you upload, and your conversations with the AI assistant. Pay, payroll and other sensitive HR and finance records are visible only to managers and owners of the workspace
  • Purchase data: your subscription status, a hashed Google Play purchase token (Android) or your Stripe customer reference (card payments on PC, web and the direct-download app). Payments are handled by Google Play or Stripe; we never receive your full card details
  • Install date: the date the Android app was first installed on your device, used only to start your 30-day free trial from that day
  • Usage and security data: counts of AI requests, AI tokens and storage used (to apply plan limits), and audit logs of actions taken in your workspace
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob (Android) and Google AdSense (PC and web) show ads. Because some users are teenagers, every ad request is marked as child-directed and under the age of consent: ads are non-personalised, limited to content suitable for all ages, and the app does not collect your device's advertising ID. Ad providers may still process limited technical data (such as IP address and ad-interaction data) to deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure them.

Voice: voice input normally uses the speech recognition built into your phone or web browser, and spoken replies use the text-to-speech built into your phone or browser; in that case the app receives only the recognised text. For languages your device does not support, the app can instead record what you say while you hold the microphone on (with your permission) and send the recording to our AI provider to turn it into text, or send a reply's text to be read aloud. These recordings and audio are processed only to answer that request and are never stored by us. Your device or browser provider may process the audio under its own privacy policy (for example, Google Chrome and many Android phones use Google's speech service). You can turn spoken replies on or off in the AI assistant at any time.

Location: only when you tap Share my location on a route, delivery or shipment, the app reads your device's location once (with your permission) and saves it on that record so your team can see where the vehicle is. The app never tracks location in the background.

We do not collect contacts, photos or files you have not chosen to upload.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and secure your account and workspace
  • To provide the app's features, including AI-assisted answers, drafts and workflows
  • To verify subscriptions with Google Play or Stripe and apply trial and plan limits
  • To run the 30-day free trial and, if you choose to subscribe, the £4.99/month subscription (see section 5)
  • To show ads: on every eligible screen during the free trial, and on about 5% of screens for paid subscribers
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and investigate problems
  • To comply with legal obligations

Lawful bases under UK GDPR: contract (providing the app), legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, service improvement), consent (personalised advertising where required) and legal obligation.

4. AI Processing

When you use the AI assistant, your request and the relevant records needed to answer it are sent to our AI provider, OpenRouter (which routes requests to the underlying AI model provider), which processes them on our behalf to generate a response. We send only the data needed for each request. The assistant replies in the language you write or speak in, or in the app language you have chosen.

Actions that change records or contact other people, for example sending an email or approving an invoice, are only carried out after a person in your workspace approves them. Prices, stock levels, totals and tax are calculated from your own records, not generated by AI. AI output can still contain mistakes, so please review drafts before approving them.

5. Free Trial and Subscription

  • Free trial (30 days, with ads): every new user gets 30 days of free use with all features. The trial starts on the day you install the Android app, or the day you sign up on PC or the web. During the free trial, ads are shown (see section 6). No payment details are needed and you are never charged automatically when the trial ends.
  • When the free trial ends: your data is kept, but the AI assistant is paused until you choose to subscribe. You will see in the app how many trial days are left.
  • Subscription (£4.99 per month): if you choose to subscribe, the subscription starts on the day you pay and renews every month until you cancel. Subscribers keep all features and see about 95% fewer ads. One subscription works on Android, PC and the web with the same account. Prices include VAT where required; Google Play may show the price in your local currency.
  • Paying: on Android through Google Play; on PC, the web and the direct-download app by card through Stripe. To subscribe by card you must confirm that you are 18 or over, or that your parent or guardian is paying and agrees.
  • Cancelling: in Google Play for Play subscriptions, or under Subscription › Manage card subscription for card payments. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.

6. Advertising

During the 30-day free trial (counted from the day you install the app, or the day you sign up on PC or the web) the app shows ads: banner ads on overview screens and occasional full-screen ads when you switch between the Home, Customers and More tabs, provided by Google AdMob on Android and Google AdSense on PC and the web. Paid subscribers see about 95% fewer ads. Ads are never shown over the AI assistant conversation, approvals, invoices or payment screens. All ads are non-personalised and child-directed (see section 12), so they are not based on your interests or activity. We never pass your business records, customer data, invoices or files to advertisers. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

7. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers acting on our behalf, under contracts that protect it:

  • OpenRouter and the AI model provider it routes to, AI processing for the assistant
  • Google, Google Play distribution and subscription billing; Google AdMob and AdSense advertising
  • Stripe, card payments for subscriptions on PC, the web and the direct-download Android app
  • Cloud hosting and database providers, running the app's backend service and storing your data securely

We may also disclose data where required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety. Your business data is visible only to members of your own workspace.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Workspaces are isolated from one another, access is controlled by roles, API keys are kept on our servers and never in the app, and important actions are recorded in an audit log. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the ICO of any qualifying breach as required by law.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: More › Settings & security › Delete my account
  • On the web: follow the steps on our account deletion page

Deleting your account removes your profile and login, and any workspace where you are the only member together with its business data and files. In shared workspaces, records you created for the business remain available to your colleagues, but your personal conversations are deleted. Backups are overwritten within 30 days. We may keep limited records where the law requires (for example, purchase records needed for tax purposes). Cancelling a subscription is done separately: in Google Play for Play subscriptions, or under Subscription › Manage card subscription for card payments.

10. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can export invoice data as CSV in the app. To exercise any right, email ali.abdalla@softwareequality.com; we respond within 30 days. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk, Tel: 0303 123 1113.

11. International Transfers

Some providers (including OpenRouter, AI model providers, Stripe and Google) process data outside the UK. Where this happens we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or Standard Contractual Clauses.

12. Age Requirement and Younger Users

Business AI Manager is intended for people aged 15 and over, such as business owners, their staff and teenagers helping to run a family business. It is not directed at children under 15.

Age check at sign-up: when an account is created we ask for the month and year of birth. This is used only to check age and is not stored. Anyone under 15 cannot create an account: no account is created, nothing they entered is kept, and that device or browser must wait 24 hours before trying again.

Protections for teenage users: we show only non-personalised, family-safe ads; do not collect the device advertising ID; do not use ads for profiling or behavioural tracking; keep the AI assistant's replies suitable for teenagers; and collect only the information needed to run the account (name, email and the business records you choose to enter).

Payments: subscriptions are paid by the account holder through Google Play or Stripe; to subscribe by card you must confirm that you are 18 or over, or that your parent or guardian is paying and agrees.

A parent or guardian can ask us at any time to review or delete a young person's data by emailing admin@softwareequality.com, or delete the account in the app under More › Settings & security › Delete my account.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will show the new 'last updated' date above and, for significant changes, let you know by email or in the app. Our general website policy is at softwareequality.com/privacy.
