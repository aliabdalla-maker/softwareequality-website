Download Business AI Manager

Your AI assistant and every business system (CRM, sales, finance, HR, supply chain and transport) on your phone and your PC. One account works everywhere.

Free for 30 days from the day you install, with ads. Then £4.99/month with 95% fewer ads. Cancel any time. One subscription covers Android, Windows and the web.

Available in 30 languages, with voice input and spoken replies.

Joining your employer? Install the app, choose "I have a join code from my employer" and type the code they gave you.

Android: Google Play

Recommended for phones and tablets. Automatic updates; pay with Google Play.

Get it on Google Play

iPhone and iPad

Install from Safari in seconds, with no App Store needed. It opens full screen from your Home Screen like any app, with the camera, microphone and files.

Install on iPhone or iPad
  1. Open this page on your iPhone or iPad in Safari and tap the button.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Business AI Manager from your Home Screen and sign in.

An App Store version is coming. Your account and data stay the same.

Windows PC

Installs from your browser in one click: no download and no Windows security warning. It opens in its own window and appears in the Start menu and taskbar like any app.

Install on your computer Download for Windows (.exe)

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway. The installer is from Equality Software Ltd.

Web app (any PC or Mac)

Nothing to install. Works in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox. In Chrome or Edge choose Install to add it to your desktop and Start menu.
