Sources and methodology

Every statistic on this site comes from a named primary source listed here. If you spot an error, email us and we will correct it.

How we use figures

  • We quote figures from the original publisher, with the date of publication, and link to it.
  • Forecasts are labelled as predictions, not facts.
  • We do not publish client results without the client’s written permission. Engagement examples on this site are illustrative and are labelled as such.
  • We review this page at least every six months. Last reviewed: October 2026.

References

  1. McKinsey & Company, The state of AI in 2025: Agents, innovation, and transformation, November 2025. www.mckinsey.com
    Used for: 88% of organisations report regular AI use in at least one business function.
  2. McKinsey & Company, Superagency in the workplace: Empowering people to unlock AI’s full potential, January 2025. www.mckinsey.com
    Used for: 1% of business leaders describe their companies as mature in AI deployment.
  3. McKinsey Global Institute, The economic potential of generative AI: The next productivity frontier, June 2023. www.mckinsey.com
    Used for: generative AI could add $2.6 trillion to $4.4 trillion a year to the global economy.
  4. Gartner, Top Strategic Technology Trends for 2025: Agentic AI, October 2024. www.gartner.com
    Used for: by 2028, 33% of enterprise software applications will include agentic AI, up from less than 1% in 2024 (a Gartner prediction).
  5. World Economic Forum, The Future of Jobs Report 2025, January 2025. www.weforum.org
    Used for: 170 million jobs created and 92 million displaced by 2030.
  6. European Union, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act), Official Journal, 12 July 2024. eur-lex.europa.eu
    Used for: application dates (Article 113), risk categories, obligations and maximum fines (Article 99).
  7. European Commission, AI Act: regulatory framework for AI (including Digital Omnibus updates), Updated regularly. digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu
    Used for: the current implementation timetable and proposed changes.
  8. ISO/IEC, ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Information technology, Artificial intelligence, Management system, December 2023. www.iso.org
    Used for: the AI management system standard our ADLC controls are aligned with.
  9. NIST, AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0), January 2023. www.nist.gov
    Used for: AI risk management practices in our governance checklist.
  10. UK Government (DSIT), A pro-innovation approach to AI regulation, March 2023, with the February 2024 response. www.gov.uk
    Used for: the five UK principles for regulators.
  11. Information Commissioner’s Office, Artificial intelligence: guidance on AI and data protection, Updated regularly. ico.org.uk
    Used for: UK GDPR expectations for AI systems.
