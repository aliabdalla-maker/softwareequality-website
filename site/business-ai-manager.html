Business AI Manager

An AI assistant for small businesses that keeps customers, leads, invoices and files in one place, and never acts without your approval.

Get notified at launch →Privacy policy
Coming soon to Google Play

What it does

Built by Equality Software using the same governance principles we bring to client projects: human approval for consequential actions, clear data boundaries and plain-English explanations.

AI business assistant

Ask questions about your customers, leads, products and invoices in plain English, by typing or by voice.

Customers and leads

Keep customer and lead records, notes and follow-ups in one place for your whole team.

Invoices and documents

Draft invoices and upload PDFs, spreadsheets and images so the assistant can work with them.

You approve every action

Anything that changes records or contacts other people, such as sending an email or approving an invoice, waits for a person in your team to approve it.

Numbers from your records

Prices, stock levels, totals and tax are calculated from your own data, not generated by AI.

Built for teams

Invite colleagues to your workspace and give each person the right role.
