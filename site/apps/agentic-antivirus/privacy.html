Privacy

Equality Software Ltd operates Agentic Antivirus. This page describes what the service collects.

What devices send

By default the agent sends security telemetry and file hashes: process names and command lines, file paths, network connection metadata, and detections. File contents are collected only if your organisation turns that on.

Voice

Read-aloud and dictation use your browser's built-in speech features. We do not record or store audio.

Advertising

During the free trial, and on unpaid plans, the console shows Google AdSense adverts and the mobile apps show Google AdMob adverts, after asking for your consent. Subscribers see 95% fewer adverts. Adverts never appear on approval, containment, threat warning, quarantine review, sign-in, payment or audit screens.

Retention

Your organisation sets how long telemetry and evidence are kept. Audit records cannot be edited or deleted.

Your rights

You can delete your account from your profile. Contact privacy@softwareequality.com for access or correction requests.
