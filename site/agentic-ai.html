What We Build

Agents That Actually Work

Every agent we build is production-hardened: monitored, tested, documented, and governed with our ADLC methodology.

Document Processing Agents

Intelligent agents that extract, classify, validate, and route documents - contracts, invoices, reports, forms - with near-human accuracy and full audit trails.

🔗

Multi-Agent Orchestration

Complex workflows handled by coordinated teams of specialist agents - planning, delegation, error recovery, and human escalation built in from the start.

💬

Conversational AI Assistants

Enterprise-grade AI assistants with deep domain knowledge, tool use capabilities, memory, and integration with your existing systems - not generic chatbots.

🔍

Research & Analysis Agents

Agents that autonomously gather information, analyse data, cross-reference sources, and produce structured reports - replacing hours of manual research work.

⚙️

Process Automation Agents

End-to-end automation of complex business processes - approval workflows, data reconciliation, report generation, customer onboarding, and compliance checks.

🧪

Code Generation Agents

AI agents that write, test, review, and deploy code - from feature implementation to bug fixes - integrated into your CI/CD pipeline with safety guardrails.
