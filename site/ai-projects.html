AI Governance Framework - Global Financial Institution

A global bank with growing AI deployment needed an enterprise governance framework ahead of the EU AI Act enforcement deadline. Existing models had no documentation, no risk classification, and no monitoring infrastructure.

We designed a complete AI governance framework covering risk-based model classification, model cards for all in-production AI systems, bias monitoring pipelines, incident response procedures, and board-level reporting templates.
