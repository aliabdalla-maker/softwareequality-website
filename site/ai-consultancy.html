What We Do

AI Consultancy That Moves the Needle

We work with CTOs, CDOs, and engineering leaders to build and execute AI strategies that deliver measurable ROI.

AI Readiness Assessment

Comprehensive audit of your people, processes, data infrastructure, and technology stack. We identify gaps, rate your AI maturity, and produce a prioritised action plan.

🗺️

AI Strategy & Roadmap

A 12-36 month AI transformation roadmap tied to your business objectives. Prioritised by ROI, feasibility, and risk - with clear milestones and success criteria.

Build vs Buy Analysis

Rigorous framework for deciding whether to build custom AI, use existing platforms, or combine both. We evaluate total cost of ownership, capability fit, and strategic positioning.

Structured evaluation of AI vendors, platforms, and tools against your specific requirements. We write the RFP, score responses, and recommend with clear rationale.

AI Investment Prioritisation

ROI modelling for AI initiatives, business case development, budget planning, and phased investment strategy - giving your CFO the rigour they need to approve AI spend.

Transformation Management

Change management for AI transformation - stakeholder communication, team upskilling, process redesign, and cultural change support to ensure adoption sticks.
